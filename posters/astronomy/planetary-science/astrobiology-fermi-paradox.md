## Introduction {.abstract}

The Milky Way has 100 to 400 billion stars and billions of rocky planets where water could be liquid, and it is three times as old as Earth. A civilisation sending probes at 1 % of light speed could reach every star in about 10 million years. Yet we see no one. In 1950 **Enrico Fermi** asked: *"Where is everybody?"*

## 1. The Drake equation {.wide}

In 1961 **Frank Drake** set out the factors that give **N**, the number of civilisations in our galaxy we could hear today:

```equation
title: The Drake equation
N = R_* \cdot f_p \cdot n_e \cdot f_l \cdot f_i \cdot f_c \cdot L
R_* | New stars formed a year in the Milky Way | 1.5 to 3
f_p | Share of stars with planets | about 1
n_e | Planets per system where water could be liquid | 0.2 to 0.4
f_l | Share of those where life begins | ?
f_i | Share of those where intelligence evolves | ?
f_c | Share of those that send signals | ?
L | Years a civilisation goes on sending | ?
```

## 2. What we have measured, and what we have not {cols=2}

```checklist

[ ] Where life begins, fl: one example, Earth

[ ] Where technology follows, fc: one example
[ ] How long it lasts, L: unknown, and ours is 80 years so far
```

## 3. Everything rests on L

Astronomy has measured the first three. The first three alone give about one new habitable world each year. Everything else rests on biology, and above all on **L**:

```logscale unit=" civilisations" title="N, if one civilisation arises every 10,000 years"
0.01 | L = 100 years: we are alone | red
1 | L = 10,000 years | amber
1000 | L = 10 million years | green
```

## 4. Earth's history as one day

If Earth's 4.54 billion years were 24 hours, microbes appear before 3 am, but animals only after 9 pm, and people in the last few seconds.

```dial24 title="4.54 billion years in 24 hours"

```

## 5. The Great Filter

If the galaxy is not full of civilisations, at least one step from bare rock to starships must be very hard. Is that step **behind us**, or **ahead**?

```flow
[Water and chemistry|green] -abiogenesis-> [Microbes|green] -2 billion years-> [Complex cells|amber] -> [Intelligence|amber] -> [Technology|blue] -the step ahead?-> [Across the stars|grey]
Life began almost as soon as Earth could hold it. Complex cells waited two billion years, for one cell to swallow another and keep it as a mitochondrion.
```

## 6. Answers to the paradox {.wide}

```compare
| No one is there | They are there, but quiet | They choose not to be seen
The idea | Microbes may be common, but complex life needs a run of rare luck | Distances are vast and loud signals brief | Advanced civilisations avoid contact, or change beyond recognition
Examples | **Rare Earth**: plate tectonics, a large Moon, a quiet Sun, all at once. **First-born**: gamma-ray bursts sterilised galaxies until recently | **A short window**: we leaked strong radio for 80 years, then moved to fibre. **Life under ice**: oceans on moons that never see the sky | **The zoo**: a policy of no contact. **Going quiet**: minds that move into small, cold computers instead of filling galaxies
```

## 7. The Kardashev scale {cols=2}

In 1964 **Nikolai Kardashev** ranked civilisations by the power they use. Carl Sagan made it continuous:

```equation
K = \frac{\log_{10} P - 6}{10}
K | Kardashev rating |
P | Power used | W
```

```logscale unit=" W"
1.9e13 | Humanity today, K = 0.73 | amber
1e16 | Type I: a planet's power | green
1e26 | Type II: a star's output | blue
1e36 | Type III: a galaxy's | purple
```

## 8. How we search

```cards cols=1 style=top
Biosignatures in air | JWST, and later the Habitable Worlds Observatory, look for gases that should not exist together: **oxygen and ozone beside methane**, or the jump in reflection at 700 nm that plants cause | green
Life in our Solar System | **Perseverance** cores Jezero Crater for return to Earth. **Europa Clipper** and **Dragonfly** look for amino acids of one handedness: life uses only left-handed ones | teal
Radio and laser SETI | Nature makes nothing narrower than a few hundred hertz. A **1 Hz signal** in the water hole, or a nanosecond laser pulse, would be made | blue
Megastructures and pollution | A **Dyson swarm** round a star would glow with waste heat at 100 to 300 K. Industrial gases such as CFCs might show in a transit | amber
```

## In short {.summary}

Rocky planets, water and organic molecules are everywhere. Whether we are alone depends on how hard the step from chemistry to minds is, and on how long minds last.

### Microbes on Europa would change everything

Life found again, on its own, would show that the first step is easy, and move the Great Filter towards us.

### Earth has signalled for 2.4 billion years

Since the Great Oxidation Event our air has held oxygen, a sign a JWST-like telescope could read from afar.

### To raise N, raise L

We cannot change how fast stars form. We can change how long we last.
